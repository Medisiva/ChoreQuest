ChoreQuest privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Contact: sivaprasad.i2@gmail.com

ChoreQuest helps a family run chores and rewards together. Parents create and manage the family account. Children never create an account, never give an email address and never chat with anyone outside their family. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

Who controls a family's data

The parent who creates a family is its owner. Parents add each child, choose the child's nickname, age group and picture, and set the child's 4-digit PIN. By adding a child, a parent confirms they are that child's parent or legal guardian and agrees to this policy on the child's behalf. Parents can review and export family information in the app. The family owner can request deletion of the whole family.

We do not collect location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising IDs or payment details. ChoreQuest contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Who can see it

Only members of the same family can see that family's information: its parents, and its children while signed in with their PIN. Security rules on our servers enforce this. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) to host the app's data, photos and sign-in. Google processes this data on our behalf under its data processing terms, in the United States.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is encrypted at rest by Google. Child PINs are stored only as hashes, and repeated wrong PINs temporarily lock the profile. Parent areas of the app need the parent's password.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Delete your account: in the app, open Profile → Family settings → Delete my account. Your sign-in, parent profile and wallet history are deleted immediately. If another parent shares the family, the family stays with them. If you are the only parent, the family is disabled at once and all of its data, including children's profiles and photos, is scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days, at the next daily cleanup run.
  • Delete the whole family: the family owner can choose Delete family data. Access stops immediately and everything is scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days, at the next daily cleanup run, during which the owner can still restore it.
  • Without the app: follow these steps or email us from your account's address.
  • Your copy: parents can export the family's data as a file from Settings.

Otherwise we keep data while the family account is active.

Children's privacy

ChoreQuest is designed for families and is used by children under parental supervision. We collect from children only what the app needs to work, as listed above, and only after a parent has created the child's profile. Parents can review their children’s profiles and activity. To correct or delete child information, contact us; the family owner can also request deletion of the whole family. If you believe a child's information was added without a parent's permission, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information and to object to processing. Most of this can be done directly in the app; for anything else, email sivaprasad.i2@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell parents in the app.
