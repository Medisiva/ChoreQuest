Delete your ChoreQuest account

Contact: sivaprasad.i2@gmail.com

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ChoreQuest and sign in as the parent.
  2. Go to Profile → Family settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. If the app asks, sign out and sign in again first: deletion needs a recent sign-in.

Without the app

Email sivaprasad.i2@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my ChoreQuest account". We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.
